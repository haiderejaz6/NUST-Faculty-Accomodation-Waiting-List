How the projected turn is calculated

Velocity. Each time a new register is added, this page compares it to the previous one, category by category. Anyone who was on a list before and isn't anymore is counted as a departure — almost always an allocation, occasionally a withdrawal or transfer between categories, which we can't fully tell apart from the register alone. Departures ÷ days between the two lists ÷ 30.44 gives allocations per month for that category. With more than one interval on file, the rate is the total departures across all intervals divided by total days — this smooths out any one unusually quiet or busy stretch.

Projected turn. For someone at rank R in a category, the estimate is R ÷ monthly rate months from the latest register date. This assumes the category keeps clearing from the front at roughly its historical pace — a reasonable first estimate, not a promise. It will get more reliable as more registers are added, and categories with only one interval of data (as now) carry a wider margin of error.

Matching people across lists. Names are matched after stripping titles (Dr, Mr, Ms, Engr…) and punctuation. A small number of spelling variants between PDFs (e.g. OCR differences) are caught with a fuzzy match against department and request date. Genuine near-duplicate names in different categories are treated as different applications, since categories are matched separately.

Adding a new register. Convert the new PDF into a dated JSON file following the same shape as the existing files in /data, add its filename to data/manifest.json, and push. No code changes needed — this page re-reads the manifest and recomputes everything on load.

Source: NUST Directorate of Accommodation waiting lists. Rankings and rates are estimates for planning purposes only and are not an official commitment from NUST.
